# Acme Robotics FAQ
*Document ID: ACME-FAQ · Last updated: 2026-09-01*

## Return window
Acme Robotics accepts returns within 14 days of delivery, no exceptions.

## Warranty
All Acme Robotics arms carry a 90-day warranty against manufacturing defects.

## Shipping
Acme Robotics ships only within the continental United States, using freight carriers for large items.

## Support contact
Contact Acme Robotics support at help@acme-robotics.example. There is no phone support.
