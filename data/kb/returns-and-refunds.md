# Returns and Refunds
*Document ID: KB-RETURNS · Last updated: 2026-09-01*

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. Gold members have 45 days and Premium members have 60 days. The window is counted from the date the carrier marks the package as delivered, not from the date you ordered.

## Condition of returned items
Returned items should be unused or like new and include the original packaging and all accessories. Opened electronics are accepted if they are not damaged by the customer, but a 15% restocking fee applies to opened electronics unless the item is defective.

## How to start a return
Open the order in your account, choose Request return, and select a reason. You will receive a return authorization (RMA) number and a prepaid shipping label by email. Write the RMA number on the box. Returns sent without an RMA number can take up to 10 extra business days to process.

## Return shipping cost
Return labels are free when the item is defective, damaged, or sent by mistake. For any other reason, the label costs $6.95, which is deducted from your refund. You may also ship the item back yourself, but we cannot reimburse the postage.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after our warehouse receives and inspects the returned item. Your bank or card issuer may need up to 3 more business days to show the refund. We do not issue refunds as cash or cheques.

## Items damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, report it within 7 days of delivery and include clear photos of the item and the box. We will send a free replacement or a full refund. For items priced under $30, no return is required and you may keep or dispose of the damaged item.

## Wrong item received
If we sent the wrong item, we will email a free return label and ship the correct item using Express shipping at no charge. Please report the mistake within 14 days of delivery.

## Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, engraved or custom keyboards, and products with a removed or damaged serial number cannot be returned. Items returned after the return window are not eligible for a refund, but they may still be covered by the warranty if they are defective.

## Exchanges
We do not offer direct exchanges. To swap an item, return the original for a refund and place a new order. This keeps the price and stock of the new item accurate at the time you buy it.

## Refunds for cancelled and refused orders
Refunds for cancelled orders arrive within 3 business days. If you refuse a delivery, the refund is issued after the package returns to our warehouse, minus the original shipping cost.

## Returning part of an order
You can return one or more items from an order with several items. Refunds are calculated per item. If the order qualified for free shipping because it was $50 or more, and the items you keep add up to less than $50, the $4.99 standard shipping fee is deducted from your refund.
