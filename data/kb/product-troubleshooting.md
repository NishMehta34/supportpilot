# Product Troubleshooting
*Document ID: KB-TROUBLESHOOT · Last updated: 2026-09-01*

## Mouse: pairing over Bluetooth
The Nimbus Glide Wireless Mouse (PRD-001) supports Bluetooth 5.0 and a 2.4 GHz USB dongle. To pair over Bluetooth, hold the pairing button on the underside for 3 seconds until the LED blinks blue, then choose "Glide Mouse" in your computer's Bluetooth settings.

## Mouse: cursor lag or dropouts
If the cursor stutters when using the dongle, move the dongle to a USB port closer to the mouse and away from USB 3.0 devices and external drives, which can cause interference. A short USB extension cable often fixes it. You can also switch to Bluetooth mode if the dongle keeps failing.

## Mouse: battery
The mouse uses one AA battery, which typically lasts about 12 months. A red blinking LED means the battery is low. A battery included in the box is covered by the warranty for 6 months only.

## Mouse: factory reset
If the mouse behaves erratically, hold the left button, right button, and scroll wheel together for 10 seconds until the LED flashes white. This removes all Bluetooth pairings, so you will need to pair the mouse again afterwards.

## Keyboard: Mac and Windows modes
The Nimbus Forge Mechanical Keyboard (PRD-002) works with both Mac and Windows. Press Fn+M for the Mac layout or Fn+W for the Windows layout. The keyboard remembers the last mode after you switch it off.

## Keyboard: keys not registering
First connect the keyboard with the USB-C cable to rule out a Bluetooth problem. If a single key still fails, the switches are hot-swappable, so you can pull the switch out and press it back in firmly. If several keys fail, contact support because this may be a warranty case.

## Keyboard: factory reset and backlight
Hold Fn+Esc for 5 seconds to restore factory settings, which also clears saved Bluetooth pairings. Backlight brightness is changed with Fn plus the up or down arrow key.

## Hub: ports and what is included
The Nimbus Dock USB-C Hub (PRD-003) is a 7-in-1 hub with one HDMI port, two USB-A 3.0 ports, an SD card slot, a microSD card slot, an Ethernet port, and a USB-C power passthrough port.

## Hub: no display on the monitor
The hub outputs 4K video over HDMI at 30 Hz, not 60 Hz. If the screen stays black, use an HDMI 2.0 cable, unplug the hub for 10 seconds, reconnect it to the computer first and then the monitor, and check that your computer's USB-C port supports video output.

## Hub: charging passthrough
The hub supports up to 100W USB-C power passthrough, but only if you connect a 100W or higher power adapter to the hub's charging port. A lower rated adapter charges more slowly, and a laptop that needs more power than supplied may drain slowly while in use.

## Hub: warm to the touch and disconnecting drives
The hub can become warm during normal use, up to about 45°C on the surface, which is expected. If external drives disconnect, turn off USB power saving in your operating system settings and connect power-hungry drives directly to the computer.

## Contacting support about a product problem
When you email support about a hardware problem, include the product ID (for example PRD-002), your order ID, and a short description or photo of the problem. This speeds up both troubleshooting and warranty cases.
