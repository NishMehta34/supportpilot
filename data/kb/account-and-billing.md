# Account and Billing
*Document ID: KB-ACCOUNT · Last updated: 2026-09-01*

## Resetting your password
On the login page choose Forgot password and enter your email. We send a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. After 5 failed login attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes. If the reset email does not arrive, check your spam folder before requesting another one.

## Changing your email address
Open Settings, then Profile, and enter the new email address. We send a 6-digit verification code to both the old and the new address, and the change takes effect only after you enter the codes. If you no longer have access to your old email, contact support and be ready to confirm your order ID and the last 4 digits of your payment card.

## Membership tiers
There are three tiers. Standard is the default for every account. Gold is unlocked automatically after you spend $500 in a 12-month period. Premium is a paid membership costing $99 per year and can be joined at any time.

## Tier benefits
Standard members get 30-day returns and email support within 48 hours. Gold members get 45-day returns, free Express shipping, and email support within 12 hours. Premium members get 60-day returns, free Express shipping, same-day order handling, and access to phone support.

## Contacting support
Email support is available at support@nimbus.example every day, and replies follow the response time of your tier. Premium phone support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm Eastern Time. We do not offer live chat.

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash, cheques, cryptocurrency, or gift cards from other companies.

## Being charged twice
When you place an order, your bank may show a temporary pending authorization as well as the final charge. Duplicate pending authorizations disappear on their own within 3–5 business days. If two completed charges appear for the same order, contact support with the order ID and we will refund the extra charge within 5 business days.

## Invoices and company details
You can download a PDF invoice from the Orders page. The company name and tax ID on an invoice can be edited within 30 days of the order date. After 30 days, contact support to request a corrected invoice.

## Premium renewal and cancellation
Premium renews automatically each year. You can cancel at any time in Settings under Membership. If you cancel within 14 days after a renewal charge, you receive a prorated refund. After cancelling, your account returns to the tier you have earned through spending.

## Deleting your account
You can request deletion under Settings, then Privacy. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Order and invoice records are kept for 7 years as required for tax purposes and are not deleted with the account.

## Two-factor authentication
You can turn on two-factor authentication in Settings under Security. When it is on, we ask for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app each time you log in from a new device. If you lose access to your authenticator app, contact support and we will disable it after an identity check.
