# Shipping Policy
*Document ID: KB-SHIPPING · Last updated: 2026-09-01*

## Shipping options and prices
We offer two shipping speeds within the United States. Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days and costs $4.99, or is free on orders of $50 or more. Express shipping takes 2–3 business days and costs $12.99. Gold members get free Express shipping on every order, and Premium members get free Express shipping plus priority handling.

## Order processing time
Orders are packed and shipped from our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio. Processing takes 1 business day. Orders placed after 2:00 pm Eastern Time are treated as placed on the next business day. Premium members' orders placed before 2:00 pm Eastern Time ship the same day.

## Tracking your order
You will receive an email with a tracking number within 24 hours after your order ships. Tracking updates from the carrier can lag by up to 12 hours, so a package may look stuck for half a day even when it is moving. You can also see tracking in your account under Orders.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address only while the order status is "processing". Once the status is "shipped", we cannot change the address, and you would need to contact the carrier directly to arrange a redirect. To change an address, open the order in your account and choose Edit address.

## Cancelling before shipment
An order can be cancelled only while its status is "processing". Open the order and choose Cancel order. Cancelled orders are refunded to the original payment method within 3 business days. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled and must be returned instead.

## Delayed or lost packages
If a Standard order has not arrived 10 business days after the ship date, or an Express order 5 business days after the ship date, contact support with your order ID. We open a carrier investigation and, once the package is confirmed lost, you can choose a free replacement or a full refund including shipping.

## International shipping
We ship outside the United States to Canada and the United Kingdom only. International orders use a flat $19.99 rate and take 8–14 business days. Customers are responsible for any import duties or taxes charged on delivery. Free-shipping thresholds and the Gold or Premium shipping perks do not apply to international orders.

## PO boxes and address types
Standard shipping can be delivered to PO boxes. Express shipping requires a street address because carriers cannot deliver express parcels to PO boxes. Please include an apartment or unit number to avoid delivery failure.

## Holiday delays
Between November 15 and December 31, carriers are busier and delivery can take 2 extra business days beyond the times shown at checkout. The lost-package timelines are extended by the same 2 days during this period.

## Undeliverable packages
If a carrier cannot deliver a package and it comes back to us, we contact you by email. You can choose a reshipment for a $4.99 fee or a refund of the item price. The original shipping cost is not refunded for packages returned because of an incorrect address.

## Delivery attempts and signatures
Carriers attempt delivery up to 3 times. Orders over $150 require a signature on delivery. If nobody is available, the carrier leaves a notice and holds the package at a local facility for 5 days before returning it to us.
