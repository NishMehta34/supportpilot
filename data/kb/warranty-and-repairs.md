# Warranty and Repairs
*Document ID: KB-WARRANTY · Last updated: 2026-09-01*

## Warranty length by product
Every Nimbus product is covered against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date. The Nimbus Glide Wireless Mouse (PRD-001) has a 1-year warranty. The Nimbus Forge Mechanical Keyboard (PRD-002) has a 2-year warranty. The Nimbus Dock USB-C Hub (PRD-003) has a 1-year warranty.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects such as a dead sensor or scroll wheel on the mouse, switches or keys that stop registering on the keyboard, and ports that stop working on the hub. Batteries that come with a product are covered for 6 months only.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover liquid damage, drops or crushing, damage from repairs done by anyone other than Nimbus, normal wear such as shiny keycaps, or loss and theft. Damage caused by using a power adapter that is not rated for the product is also excluded.

## How to make a warranty claim
Email support with your order ID and photos or a short video showing the problem. We reply within 2 business days. A support agent may ask you to try basic troubleshooting first. Warranty claims are not limited by the return window, so you can claim long after 30 days as long as the product is still under warranty.

## Replacement process
If your claim is approved, we ship a replacement within 2 business days. You must send the defective unit back within 14 days using the prepaid label we provide. If the defective unit is not returned within 14 days, we charge the price of the replacement to your payment method.

## Warranty on replacements
A replacement unit is covered for the rest of the original warranty period, or for 90 days from the day you receive it, whichever is longer.

## Repairs outside of warranty
For the keyboard, we offer a flat-fee switch replacement service of $19 per keyboard, plus shipping. The mouse and the hub cannot be repaired. If a mouse or hub is out of warranty, we email you a 15% discount code for buying a new one.

## Gifts and transfers
Warranty coverage stays with the product. If you received a product as a gift, you are covered, but you need the original order ID to make a claim. We do not require product registration.

## Warranty regions
The warranty is valid for products bought from Nimbus Gadgets and shipped to the United States, Canada, or the United Kingdom. Products bought from other retailers are not covered by this warranty.

## Firmware updates
The keyboard's firmware can be updated through the Nimbus Companion app for Mac and Windows. Firmware updates are free, and updating the firmware does not void the warranty.
